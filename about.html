Element Shot

Version … · free and open

Screenshot exactly one element of a web page, dropped onto a soft pastel background with a drop shadow. No accounts, no tracking, no network.

How to use

  1. Click the Element Shot icon in the toolbar.
  2. Move the mouse; the element under the cursor is outlined.
  3. Click it. Hold Shift while clicking for a transparent PNG with no background. Press Esc to cancel.
  4. The PNG lands in your Downloads folder and on your clipboard, ready to paste.

Rounded cards keep their corners, and a transparent container such as a row of cards is cut into its visible parts so the background shows through the gaps.

Chrome does not allow screenshots of its own pages or the Web Store; the icon shows a red ! there. For local files, turn on "Allow access to file URLs" for Element Shot at chrome://extensions.

Privacy

Element Shot only touches a page when you click its icon. The captured image is composed on your computer and saved to your Downloads folder. Nothing is uploaded, stored, or collected. Privacy policy
