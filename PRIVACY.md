# Element Shot privacy policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026

Element Shot is a Chrome extension that saves a screenshot of a single element on a web page.

## What it does with your data

- **Nothing leaves your computer.** The screenshot is captured, composed, and saved locally. The extension makes no network requests and has no server.
- **No collection.** Element Shot does not collect, store, or transmit personal data, browsing history, page content, or analytics of any kind.
- **No accounts.** There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to log in to.

## Permissions and why they are needed

- `activeTab` and `scripting`: to run the element picker on the current tab, and only after you click the extension icon.
- `downloads`: to save the finished PNG to your Downloads folder.

The extension has no host permissions and cannot read pages you have not clicked its icon on.
